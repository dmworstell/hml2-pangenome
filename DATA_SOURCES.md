# Public data sources

The derived-data archive records the retained input files and their checksums.
Sample and source-interval identifiers are preserved in the catalog and
sequence headers. Whole-genome assemblies and raw reads remain at their source
repositories rather than being duplicated in this release.

| Input | Source |
|---|---|
| Human pangenome assemblies and graphs | Human Pangenome Reference Consortium, https://humanpangenome.org/ and https://github.com/human-pangenomics |
| HGSVC assemblies | Human Genome Structural Variation Consortium, https://www.hgsvc.org/ |
| Population/sample metadata and high-coverage short reads | International Genome Sample Resource, https://www.internationalgenome.org/ |
| MAGE expression matrices and covariates | https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10535719. Source RNA-seq BioProject PRJNA851328 |
| GEUVADIS expression | ArrayExpress/BioStudies E-GEUV-1, matrix GEUVADIS_gene_RPKM_50FN_resk10.txt.gz |
| EBV phenotypes, Mandage et al. | https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0179446.s003 |
| EBV phenotypes, Houldcroft et al. | https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0108384.s003 |
| Intrinsic growth, Im et al. | https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1002525.s001 |
| Anti-CD20 phenotypes, Small et al. | Supplementary data at https://doi.org/10.3390/cells12121574 |

The recurrent-conversion analysis uses published rate estimates from Dumont
and Eichler (2013) and Harpak et al. (2017). The exact rate values and source
labels are in Table S13 and its analysis code.

own terms and citations. No license here supersedes the source-dataset terms.
